About The Grand Fest

PRAVAH is the flagship annual national-level techno-cultural festival of Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan (SKIT), Jaipur. Bringing together technical mastery, expressive art forms, and electric performances, this 3-day extravaganza sets the ultimate stage for young talent across the country.
