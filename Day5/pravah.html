About the Fest

Where Heritage Meets Innovation

Pravah — meaning "flow" — is SKIT's annual fest where Rajasthan's living heritage meets tomorrow's technology. Over seven days, the campus turns into a stage for Ghoomar and Kalbeliya dancers, Kathputli puppeteers, hackathon coders, and robot builders alike.

One campus, two worlds, one celebration — come be part of the flow.
