About PRAVAH 2026

Welcome to PRAVAH

PRAVAH 2026 is the annual college fest of Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology (SKIT), Jaipur. The fest brings together talented students from different colleges to compete, innovate and celebrate through exciting technical and cultural events.

From Coding Competitions to Robotics, Gaming, Workshops and Cultural Performances, PRAVAH has something for everyone.
